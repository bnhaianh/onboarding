COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

What does AI visibility mean?

AI visibility describes how often a brand appears in answers to a defined set of questions. A useful report should also show which prompts were checked, which AI engine was used and the underlying responses.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in search results. AI visibility analysis examines whether answer engines mention, recommend or cite your brand in generated responses. They complement each other.

Which AI platforms are covered?

The product preview illustrates analysis across AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI. The scope of a personalized audit is confirmed with you first.

What will I receive in an audit?

An audit can include the questions checked, brand and competitor mentions, cited sources and opportunities to improve. The final scope is confirmed before the assessment.

Can I choose the topics to track?

Yes. Start with the questions and attributes most relevant to your customers; the demo shows how topics and prompts can be organized.

Can a brand guarantee a mention in AI answers?

No. AI answers vary by platform, prompt and time. Reviewing visibility and improving useful, accessible information can help you understand opportunities, but cannot guarantee a recommendation.
